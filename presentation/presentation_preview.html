1. Motivation & Problem Statement
1. The Prefetching Dilemma
  • Aggressive Speculation: Prefetchers (AMPM, IP-Stride) boost regular patterns but flood LLC on pointer-chasing patterns (429.mcf).
  • Policy Blindness: Standard LRU/SRRIP policies treat speculative prefetches with high priority, evicting demand data.
  • Result: Heavy LLC thrashing & IPC degradation.
2. Limitation of Prior Art
  • DRRIP: Requires PSEL set-dueling counters.
  • SHiP: Requires an 8,192-entry SHCT table.
  • Research Goal: Achieve prefetch resilience with 0 extra storage bits.

2. Proposed Architecture
Key Architectural Rules
  • Demand Fills: Inserted at MRU / RRPV=2 (standard priority).
  • Prefetch Fills: Demoted to LRU / RRPV=3 (distant priority).
  • Useful Hits: Promoted to MRU / RRPV=0 upon hit.
  • Zero Overhead: Uses ChampSim's access_type::PREFETCH hint (0 extra storage bytes).
